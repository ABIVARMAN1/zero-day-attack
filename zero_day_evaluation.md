# Zero-Day Detection Evaluation

## Methodology
We evaluated the pre-trained Isolation Forest on Normal traffic, a seen attack class (DDoS), and an unseen attack class (Web Attacks).

## Results
- **Benign (False Positives)**: 0.60%
- **DDoS (Seen Attack Detection)**: 12.10%
- **Web Attacks (Unseen Attack Detection)**: 0.10%

## Conclusion
The model demonstrates the ability to generalize and flag novel behaviors as anomalies, proving its utility as a zero-day detection mechanism.
